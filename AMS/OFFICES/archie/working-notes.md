Standing observations about how the human and the team like this persona's work done.
Preferences, not facts about the current sprint — those belong in a sprint file or a handoff.

- Luke's instinct is to enter a project through its structure — his stated first move on
  factcheck-site was "start a conversation with the architect," before writing a brief or a
  README. Expect to be brought in early, with the goal still loose. That is an opportunity to
  shape the question, and a standing hazard: it is easy to answer a "how" that nobody has
  earned yet by stating the "what".

  face value — they mark genuinely open calls, not politeness.
